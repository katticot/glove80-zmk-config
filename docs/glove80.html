Update your keyboard · physical bootloader shortcuts

Use the same combined UF2 on both halves. These are fixed switch locations, even if you have moved your keycaps.

  1. Right first: power off, connect USB, hold positions (original I + PgDn; current U + PgDn), then power on. Copy your UF2 to GLV80RHBOOT.
  2. Left: power off, connect USB, hold positions (original Magic + E; current Magic + F), then power on. Copy the same UF2 to GLV80LHBOOT.
  3. Wait for each drive to disappear before disconnecting. Magic is at the bottom-left outer position; PgDn is at the bottom-right outer position.

Layout verification & source

80 positions per layer, 40 per half, all 26 letters once on the base layer. Geometry includes MoErgo’s column offsets and thumb rotations. Position numbers are zero-based ZMK binding indices; matrix coordinates are electrical addresses, not MoErgo’s printed C/R names.

Home-row holds: 280 ms tapping term, 175 ms quick-tap, opposite-hand trigger lists. Thumb layer-taps: 200 ms. Magic: tap for RGB status, hold for layer 2. Layer 1 replaces the base Up/Down keys with Home/End.

This is a source snapshot, not a reading of the keyboard’s installed firmware. A successful build does not confirm every physical switch or resolve a wireless freeze.
