Layout verification & source

80 positions per layer, 40 per half, all 26 letters once on the base layer. Geometry includes MoErgo’s column offsets and thumb rotations. Position numbers are zero-based ZMK binding indices; matrix coordinates are electrical addresses, not MoErgo’s printed C/R names.

Home-row holds: 290 ms tapping term, with per-finger times (pinky 310, ring 290, middle 260, index 220); chord holds 200 ms. Thumb layer holds: 250 ms. Sticky one-shot keys: 250 ms. Tapping resolution 200 ms (difficulty level 4). Home-row keys also hold their own finger layer, so a one-handed hold falls back to a plain tap when ENFORCE_BILATERAL is set in the keymap.

This is a source snapshot, not a reading of the keyboard’s installed firmware. A successful build does not confirm every physical switch or resolve a wireless freeze.
